I'd like to begin this message not with an apology, but with a sincere expression of my deepest gratitude for everyone who has been a part of this webring.

Computering started in early 2025 as a small little thing between me and my friends. To tell the truth, I found out how easy it was to set up one of these using onionring.js, and immediately threw something together in just a few hours.

I never expected anyone besides my fellow webdev friends to be genuinely interested in this webring, but I left a public join button in anyhow, just in case. Sure enough, after a few weeks, requests started coming in. I was overjoyed that anyone else wanted to be a part of this little community, to prominently affiliate themselves with my website, to something I made. And so, bit by bit over the past year, computering amassed eight total members (not counting me or my friends).

But, of course, things change. As time marched on I found my offline responsibilities growing steadily. Work, family, and other commitments began to take up more and more of my time, and I gradually began to notice that doing certain types of Website Stuff™ began to feel more like a chore and less like a hobby. Without exposing my personal matters too much, I suffer from ADHD and Autism, which means I often have a hard time managing my energy and focus. But web development has always been a safe haven for me. Making websites for whatever dumb thing I was into that week or just... Expressing myself, allowing myself to truly unmask and geek out is something I can safely say I truly love, so to see myself burning out like this was very upsetting.

But even besides my own mental health concerns, what really made me stop and evaluate if it was even worth committing to this project was how I began slowly losing my grip on its moderation. At first, I would thoroughly screen every join request, analizing every nook and cranny of each site to make sure they were completely safe for everyone. But as time went on and I grew more and more tired, I admittedly got less thorough with my evaluations. When I caught myself about to let a group of sites in without even so much as looking them over today, I decided it was time to retire this project.

Above all else, before self expression, creativity and artistic integrity, what I value most in this world is the safety and well-being of my fellow people. And with the polarized and violent state of today's world, it's more important than ever to make sure you aren't platforming harmful content.

Thankfully, nothing like that has happened yet. All 9 other sites in this webring belong to wonderful people deserving of your attention and love, but I can't assure you that this would continue to be true if I kept moderating this project.

So thank you all once again for being a part of this little community while it lasted. I had tons of fun and got to see so many beautiful creations made by passionate developers because of it, and for that I will be forever grateful.

TL;DR: Computering has been shut down due to moderation concerns and my declining ability to manage the project effectively.

Thank you for your continued support.

- sosumi

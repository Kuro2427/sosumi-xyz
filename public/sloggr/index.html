Tired of social media but can't stop posting? Statuses just don't feel the same? Check out...

sloggr!

What?

sloggr is a microblogging tool that is fully compatible with static web hosts. Useful for sharing quick updates, shower thoughts, blogposting of all sorts, really.

Why?

Slogs as a concept actually predate sloggr itself by almost a whole year, I started using them to get away from social media. Any time I had the urge to post something, I just added it to a little scrollable div on my website instead, bt that quickly proved to be too cumbersome as my site grew, so, inspired by the drawbox project, I made this funky little script to make slogging easier than ever.

How?

Through the power of using Google's suite for dubious purposes! Like any good programmer has done at least once.

And what's with the name?

sloggr comes from "slog", as mentioned above, and slog is a portmanteau of "short" and "log" therefore, a short log! ... It's a shitty name, I know, I use it half ironically myself, you can call it whatever you want, honestly.

How to use

Setting up sloggr is a shockingly easy process, all you need to get it up and running is a google account, and once you're done, you're pretty much set! Basically all you have to do is...

  1. Create a new blank google form. Feel free to set the title and description to whatever you want, this will not be publicly accessible.
  2. Change the default question to "paragraph" (or "short answer" if you want a smaller word limit, either one works)
  3. Click the big purple "publish" button.
  4. Change the form's responder setting from "anyone with the link" to "restricted". If you ignore this step, nosy people may be able to make public posts without your permission.
  5. Copy your form's responder link an keep it somewhere easy to access. This is how you will make all of your posts.
  6. Tab over to the "responses" section and click "link to sheets".
  7. Select "create a new spreadsheet" and click "create". A new window will open with your newly created responses spreadsheet, this sheet is also where you will be able to manage, edit and delete all of your posts.
  8. Click on the share button, and change the "general access" permission to "anyone with the link". Make sure the role is set to "viewer", or else much like in step 4 anyone will be able to edit your posts.
  9. Download sloggr.html, place it in your website's directory and open it in your text editor of choice.
  10. Change the "sheetId" constant to your spreadsheet's ID, which is literally just the long string of characters after /d/and before /edit in the URL you are at when editing yor sheet. E.G: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ztRsiAWRhyuiHznP5L4iA-omwV3G2mkepgqFOAFjLrA/edit
  11. Add an iframe or embed that links to your sloggr.html file and you're done! You can now freely post to your heart's content!

Optional steps

Through the power of the public bluesky api and Google App Scripts, we can make it so sloggr cross posts to everyone's third favourite social network. Useful if your stupid and dumb friends don't constantly check your site for updates (smh)

  1. Open the spreadsheet that's linked to your form responses.
  2. Click on Extensions > Apps Script.
  3. Delete the default function and paste this code into the editor.
  4. Save your changes with Ctrl+S.
  5. Switch to the "Project settings" tab (the cog icon on the side bar).
  6. Add two new script properties, one titled BSKY_HANDLE and another titled BSKY_APP_PASSWORD.
  7. Paste your bluesky handle (e.g: johndoe.bsky.app) into BSKY_HANDLE's "Value" field.
  8. Paste your bluesky account's password into the BSKY_APP_PASSWORD's "Value" field (don't worry, by doing it this way, your password is well obscured and inacessible to outsiders) and click on "Save Script Properties".
  9. Tab over to "Triggers" (the alarm clock icon on the sidebar).
  10. Create a new trigger.
  11. Change the "Select event type" property from "On open" to "On form submit".
  12. Change "Failure notification settings" to "Notify me immedietely" (optional, it's just nicer to get the email right away if something breaks).
  13. Save your settings and wait for the login prompt to open.
  14. A very scary looking message will appear once you log in telling you that Google hasn't verified this app. But the code is all right here, so if you wish to proceed, click on Advanced > Go to [PROJECT NAME].
  15. Finish the authorization as normal. Your slogs should now cross post to bluesky automatically! :D
